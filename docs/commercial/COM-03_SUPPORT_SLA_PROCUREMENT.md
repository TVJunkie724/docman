# COM-03 Support SLA Procurement

## 2026 Vault/Cloud Applicability

Local support covers installation, supported platforms, encrypted
backup/restore and diagnostics without remote document access. Cloud support
also covers account/device recovery, entitlement, quota, migration,
grace/read-only, retention dates and service incidents. Support may not promise
recovery that conflicts with VC-02/VC-07/VC-08, access document content by
default or extend Cloud retention outside the accepted policy.

## Required Decisions

- Support channels.
- SLA levels.
- Escalation.
- Enterprise security questionnaire owner.
- DPA/procurement package.
- Status page and incident communications.
- Explicit consumer support window versus any later enterprise SLA.
- Exit assistance and escalation deadlines before retention deletion.

## Stop Rules

- Stop if enterprise sales promise SLA or compliance evidence without owner and
  operational backing.
- Stop paid Cloud launch until OQ-006 and VC-04 are closed for the offered plan.
