# COM-04 Enterprise Admin Contracts

## 2026 Scope Boundary

Enterprise organizations, delegated administration and contractual policy
controls are deferred and are not part of the consumer Commercial Core unless
separately activated. A later organization model must not silently redefine
consumer Vault authority, household/profile membership, encryption ownership,
portability or deletion. Organization offboarding still requires export,
retention and explicit transfer/deletion ownership.

## Required Decisions

- Organization/account model.
- Admin roles.
- Contract ownership.
- Policy configuration.
- Offboarding.
- Data export and deletion at contract end.
- Separation of billing administrator, Vault administrator, data subject and
  support/operator privileges.

## Stop Rules

- Stop if enterprise account behavior lacks admin, offboarding and data-return
  rules.
- Stop if enterprise scope is introduced without separate product, security,
  identity, legal, backend and frontend issues.
