# REG-08 Store Platform Compliance

## Purpose

Defines platform/store requirements for distribution and review.

## Required Decisions

- Target stores and distribution channels.
- Store policy source and checked date per platform.
- Signing, notarization and packaging requirements.
- Permission declarations and privacy/data safety disclosures.
- Account deletion, subscription/payment and external purchase rules.
- Review access, demo accounts and synthetic review data.
- Beta/test distribution and staged rollout policy.

## Stop Rules

- Stop if a store/platform release lacks current policy check, permission and
  privacy disclosures, review access plan and signing/notarization decision.
