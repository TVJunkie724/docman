# REG-01 GDPR Privacy Compliance

## Purpose

Defines GDPR/DSGVO obligations for data processed by Mappm.

## Required Decisions

- Data classes and special-category data.
- Controller/processor roles.
- Legal basis per processing purpose.
- Processor agreements and subprocessors.
- Data subject rights: access, export, correction, deletion, restriction.
- Retention and deletion guarantees.
- International transfers and safeguards.
- DPIA/DSFA need.
- Breach response and notification owner.

## Product Requirements

- Privacy by design/default.
- Synthetic fixtures and screenshots.
- User-safe export/delete flows.
- Redacted logs and diagnostics.
- Consent/legal copy where required.

## Stop Rules

- Stop if personal or sensitive data is processed without legal basis,
  retention, export/delete path and logging/diagnostics boundary.
