# REG-05 NIS2 Enterprise Security

## Purpose

Defines whether NIS2-driven customer or sector requirements affect product
design, operations or contracts.

## Required Decisions

- Target customer sectors.
- Essential/important entity customer exposure.
- Security governance and supplier obligations.
- Incident reporting support expectations.
- Audit and evidence requirements.
- Contract/SLA implications.

## Stop Rules

- Stop if enterprise/security-sensitive customers are targeted without NIS2
  applicability review and supplier-security boundary.
