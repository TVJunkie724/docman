# REG-07 Product Liability Terms Support

## Purpose

Defines the promises the product makes to customers and the legal/support
boundaries around those promises.

## Required Decisions

- Terms of service and acceptable use.
- Warranty/support boundaries.
- AI/OCR disclaimers and human responsibility.
- Enterprise procurement requirements.
- Support SLA alignment.
- Product liability and safety warnings.

## Stop Rules

- Stop if marketing, onboarding or support promises behavior not backed by
  product capability, compliance review and support process.
